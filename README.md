# Ömür Gökyar

Founder & CEO of [Sanug Teknoloji](https://github.com/sanugteknoloji), a software company based in Fethiye, Türkiye.

Ömür Gökyar has been building software for more than twenty years. He leads Sanug Teknoloji's work in custom software development, SaaS platforms, game development and mobile applications, together with a dedicated Web3 and blockchain practice and AI integration for products and business workflows.

His current focus is location-based and augmented reality products, with server-side logic and security designed in from the start. Antiq World, Sanug's location-based mobile game, is in the design stage.

In Web3, his work covers smart contracts, token design, NFT, DeFi and GameFi. His first blockchain project, [Altın Lira](https://github.com/AltinLiraXAL), is an ERC-20 token indexed to the gram price of gold.

He holds a degree in Public Finance from Istanbul University, Faculty of Political Sciences, and brings a background in foreign trade and real estate to product and business decisions.

## Technology

Unity · C# · TypeScript · React · PostgreSQL · Supabase · Cloudflare · GIS and map tile pipelines · Solidity
